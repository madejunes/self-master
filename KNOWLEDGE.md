# Durable Knowledge

This file stores important concepts and insights that are worth retaining
beyond individual sessions.

It is **not** a transcript.

## Format

For each durable insight, record:

- Concept
- Explanation in the learner's own words where possible
- Why it matters
- Example/application
- Source(s)
- Confidence/limitations

## Evidence Standard

When an entry makes an academic or factual claim, record a real source.
Prefer primary research, peer-reviewed literature, standards, authoritative
institutional sources, and established textbooks as appropriate.
