# Program Decisions

## 2026-08-30 — Program Direction

### Decision

Create a self-directed master's-level program centered on Software Engineering, with Quality Engineering and AI Engineering as major areas.

### Rationale

These areas align with the learner's stated goal of becoming highly capable and provide a coherent progression from traditional software engineering through software quality into intelligent software systems.

### Evidence / Reference

The structure is consistent with established graduate software-engineering curriculum guidance, particularly ACM/IEEE-CS GSwE2009 and Carnegie Mellon Software Engineering Institute materials.

## 2026-08-30 — Project-Driven Learning

### Decision

Use realistic projects as a major mechanism for learning.

### Rationale

The learner prefers learning by doing and wants projects connected to real-world and current problems.

## 2026-08-30 — Academic Evidence

### Decision

Use substantial real academic and authoritative material.

### Requirements

* Do not hallucinate references.
* Verify citations.
* Prefer primary/peer-reviewed/official sources where appropriate.
* Use books and high-quality educational material for foundations.
* Use current literature for rapidly changing areas such as AI.

## 2026-08-30 — Persistent Memory

### Decision

GitHub repository is the source of truth; conversations are working sessions.

### Rationale

The program may span a long period and conversations may become fragmented.

A version-controlled record provides continuity and preserves decisions, progress, and evidence.

## 2026-08-30 — Initial Mode

### Decision

Remain in PLAN mode until the learner explicitly chooses to begin.

## 2026-09-03 — Planning Closure

### Decision

The initial high-level planning phase is complete enough to begin diagnostic assessment.

### Rationale

The program now has defined:

* target educational level;
* career direction;
* primary competency areas;
* supporting CS foundations;
* assessment philosophy;
* mastery levels;
* project philosophy;
* academic evidence policy;
* program modes;
* potential project direction;
* diagnostic purpose.

Further high-level planning before the diagnostic would risk designing a curriculum around assumptions about the learner's knowledge.

### Consequence

The next substantive phase is the diagnostic.

Detailed competency mapping and curriculum sequencing will be performed after diagnostic evidence is available.

## 2026-09-03 — Diagnostic-First Transition

### Decision

Use a practical, master's-oriented diagnostic as the next major learning activity.

### Rationale

The learner has substantial professional software-engineering experience but reports weak or rusty CS foundations.

A conventional knowledge exam would therefore risk both:

* underestimating practical capability; and
* overestimating gaps caused by forgotten academic material.

The diagnostic should use realistic engineering problems and distinguish conceptual understanding, practical competence, analytical ability, and higher-level evaluation.

### Planned Diagnostic Areas

* System design
* Quality engineering
* Production troubleshooting
* CS foundations
* AI engineering/evaluation
* Trade-off reasoning
* Technical explanation and defense

## 2026-09-03 — Just-in-Time Foundations

### Decision

Do not force a complete undergraduate CS curriculum before advanced engineering work.

### Rationale

The learner already has a computing degree and substantial professional experience.

Foundational material should be refreshed or learned deeply where diagnostic results and later engineering problems demonstrate that it is necessary.

## 2026-09-03 — Master's-Level Evidence

### Decision

Competence must be demonstrated through evidence rather than topic completion.

### Rationale

The target is master's-level capability, not completion of a reading list.

Evidence may include:

* explanations;
* system designs;
* implementations;
* experiments;
* debugging investigations;
* technical analyses;
* research synthesis;
* project artifacts;
* independent investigations.

## 2026-09-03 — Potential Flagship Project

### Decision

Keep a QA automation/assistance system as a candidate flagship project.

### Status

**Not committed.**

### Rationale

The learner's real engineering environment has limited dedicated QA capacity, making this a potentially high-value problem.

However, the project must first be validated from the problem side rather than being selected because AI or automation is fashionable.

The intended sequence is:

> problem → evidence → requirements → design → implementation → measurement

## 2026-09-03 — Program Modes

### Decision

Use five operating modes:

* PLAN
* LEARN
* BUILD
* RESEARCH
* CAPSTONE

### Rationale

Different activities require different working modes.

PLAN should be used for curriculum and project design.

LEARN should be used for acquiring knowledge.

BUILD should be used for practical engineering.

RESEARCH should be used for evidence-driven investigation.

CAPSTONE should represent the final independent master's-level work.

## 2026-09-03 — Current Transition

### Decision

Remain technically in **PLAN mode**, but stop expanding the high-level planning architecture.

### Next Action

Begin the diagnostic at the next substantive session.

The learner has indicated an intention to start around this weekend.
